# Backup Policy Template

## Purpose
The purpose of this backup policy is to ensure that all critical data is regularly backed up and can be restored in the event of data loss.

## Scope
This policy applies to all data stored by the organization.

## Backup Frequency
Backups shall be performed:  
- Daily at 2 AM UTC  
- Weekly on Sundays at 3 AM UTC  

## Backup Retention
Backups shall be retained for a period of 30 days. Older backups should be automatically deleted.

## Backup Method
Backups will be conducted using automated scripts that ensure data integrity and security.  
- Full backups weekly  
- Incremental backups daily

## Data Restoration
In case of data loss, the restoration process will be initiated within 24 hours. The IT department is responsible for executing the restoration procedures.

## Review
This policy shall be reviewed annually or after significant changes to the IT infrastructure.
